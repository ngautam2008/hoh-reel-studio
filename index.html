Shooting tips

Better clips, better reels

  • Hold the phone upright (vertical) and keep it steady.
  • Shoot in daylight or a well-lit place.
  • Take 6–10 short clips of 3–10 seconds each, plus a few photos.
  • Get a "before" and an "after" shot if you can. They work best.
  • Don't add music or text yourself. We'll do it.

FAQ

Questions

Can I just send an Instagram link of a reel I like?

Yes, send the link so we know what you mean, but please also send a screen recording of it. We use it only to match the style. We never reuse anyone else's video or music.

How long does it take?

Usually within 24 hours of receiving all your clips (12 hours on the Business pack). Changes take a few hours more.

What if I don't like the preview?

Tell us what to change and we'll redo it once for free. If it still isn't right, you don't pay.

Which businesses is this for?

Car care, salons, cafés, gyms, shops, real estate, events. Anything you can film on a phone.

Is the music safe to use?

Yes. We use our own music or music licensed for social media, so your reel won't be muted or taken down for copyright.

Who owns the reel?

You do. Post it anywhere: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status.
